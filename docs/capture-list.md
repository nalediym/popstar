# Capture list: what to film for "In the Loop (Devagar)"

Record everything with the recorder (`bun recorder/server.ts`, or the "recorder" entry in the browser pane).
Type the **name** below into the clip-name box so files land as `clips/<name>.mp4`.
"Song: on" = keep the **play song** box checked (it auto-stops at the end of the song).

## Setup (once, before any take)
- **Earbuds in.** The song must not bleed into the mic.
- **Light from the front.** Face a window or a warm lamp, about 45° to one side and slightly above eye level.
  No bright window behind you; backlight turns your face into a silhouette and DWPose loses your hands.
- **Camera at eye level or slightly above, landscape**, about an arm's length away. Head to waist in frame.
- **Both hands must stay inside the frame** for hand moves. If a hand leaves the frame, the pose guide loses it.
- **Keep your hands off your mouth while you sing a line.** The lip-sync reads your mouth; a hand in front of it
  (like in chunk-01) blurs the words.
- Wear something fitted, in a solid or simple color. Hair however you want to look in the video.

## Provisional hand cues (final version comes with the shot list)
Beat numbers count from the downbeat; 92 BPM ≈ 0.65s per beat.
- **"I'm in the loop"**: draw the circle with one finger.
- **"devagar"** (slowly): one slow palm roll, eyes down then back up to the lens.
- **"eu sei onde está"**: hand to heart.
- **"Keep me in the loop, meu bem"**: reach toward the lens, then pull back to your chest.
- **Snaps on beats 2 and 4** through the verse, small and low.
